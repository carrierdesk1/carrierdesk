Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information CarrierDesk (operated by M&H Dispatch Solutions) collects, how we use it and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, WhatsApp number and password (stored securely by our login provider; we never see your plain password).
  • Plan and usage: your plan status, expiry date and number of free searches used.
  • AI assistant: the questions you type and the recent chat messages sent with them.
  • Search and lookup requests: the MC/DOT numbers, filters, places and dates you enter, which are sent to our server to return results.
  • Data saved in your browser: leads, tracker entries, saved searches, email templates and document details are stored in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. We do not receive them unless a feature says so.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and errors, kept by our hosting provider.

2. Carrier information shown in the tool

Carrier names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and officers shown in CarrierDesk come from public US government records (FMCSA). We do not collect this from you. If you are a carrier and want your listing corrected, contact the FMCSA, which is the source of the record.

3. How we use information

  • To run your account, apply your plan and free-trial limits and provide support.
  • To return search results, answer AI questions and calculate routes and fuel costs.
  • To contact you about your account or plan (for example on WhatsApp or email).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Services we share data with

To work, CarrierDesk uses these providers, and the relevant data is sent to them:

  • Supabase: login, profiles and the carrier database.
  • Vercel: website hosting and server functions.
  • Anthropic: your AI assistant questions are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate answers.
  • US FMCSA / data.transportation.gov and US EIA: carrier lookups and diesel prices (carrier numbers you search, not your identity).
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, map tiles) and OSRM: the places you enter in the fuel/route calculator are sent for geocoding and routing; map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare): loads the map library.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage to keep you logged in and to save your leads, settings and templates. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your account information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time (contact below). Data saved in your browser can be removed by clearing your browser data or using the delete buttons in the app.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

CarrierDesk is for business use by adults and is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. International use

Our team is based in Pakistan, and our providers process data in other countries, including the United States. By using CarrierDesk you understand your data may be transferred and processed there.

10. Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or stop being contacted, by messaging us below.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
