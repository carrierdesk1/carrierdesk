Daily targets

Every team member gets a daily target. Their progress fills in automatically when they email or call carriers in CarrierDesk. The day follows US Eastern Time (resets at 12:00 AM ET). Members without a custom target use the default (100 emails + 100 calls).
Weekly report

Mon–Sat (US Eastern). A day counts when the member reaches both the email and call target. Only finished days count in the verdict (today shows as in progress).

Team

Team members and admins each keep their own separate leads; only the owner sees everyone's leads in Team Leads. Other signups are customers on the Free plan; search their email below and press +30 days after you receive their payment.

Team Leads

Leads of your team members and the other admins. Every person's list is separate. This page is read-only; your own leads are in My Leads.

Verify Emails

Paste emails (one per line or separated by commas). Checks syntax, duplicates, disposable / no-reply addresses and whether the domain can receive mail. It cannot confirm that a single mailbox exists.

Skip List

Tutorials

Short guides for each feature. Tap a title to open it.

Find carriers fast (Bulk search)

Pick MC range or Date range. Add filters (state, city, trucks, equipment, cargo, has email / has phone). Press Fetch carriers. Use the 7 / 30 / 90 day buttons or "New carriers (yesterday → today)" for fresh carriers.

Check a single carrier

Type an MC, DOT or company name in the top search box. You get authority, insurance, fleet, safety, contact details and cargo types. Always confirm on FMCSA/SAFER before booking.

Select, save and export

Tick the rows you want, then use Save to leads or Export to Excel (CSV). Use "Hide saved / contacted / skip list" so you do not see the same carriers again.

My Leads and follow-ups

Saved carriers appear in My Leads. Set a status, add notes and a follow-up date. Use the call / WhatsApp / copy-email buttons to reach out quickly.

Email carriers

Select carriers and press Email carriers. Write your subject and message with {first} {company} {mc} {city}. Each click opens one ready email in Gmail, then moves to the next carrier. Carriers on your Skip List are removed automatically.

Verify Emails

Paste a list of emails (or load them from your leads / bulk results). We remove bad syntax, duplicates, disposable and no-reply addresses, and check that the domain can receive mail. Copy or download the good ones.

Skip List

Add emails you never want to contact again (do not email, already booked, wrong number). You can import everyone you already contacted. Skip-listed carriers are hidden from the Email carriers queue.

Carrier Tracker and Documents

Tracker: save a carrier location/ZIP that they share with you. Documents: get the FMCSA authority page and W-9 for any MC or DOT.

AI Assistant and Fuel calculator

Ask vetting, rate or message questions in the AI Assistant (it has no live rates). The Fuel tab gives diesel prices and route miles with fuel cost.

Dark mode, profile and problems

Open the menu at the top right: Dark mode, Edit profile (name, WhatsApp, password). If something is broken, use Report Issue.
